Plan Before You Request

How to plan a custom crystal bracelet in five steps

These practical choices help Auralis recommend a suitable direction before making a quote. Photos on this site are style references; final materials and availability are confirmed for each order.

1. Choose your storyEveryday style, a gift, or a personal milestone?

Describe the occasion or feeling you want to remember. The design can symbolize a fresh start or daily reminder without promising a health outcome.

2. Pick a color directionUse a real reference photo

Tell us which colors to keep and which to change. A reference image communicates your taste, not a verified list of stones or an identical made-to-order product.

3. Compare bead sizesDelicate, balanced, or statement styling?

Bead diameter changes the visual scale and wearing experience. Compare 6mm, 8mm and 10mm bracelet styles before deciding.

4. Check wrist fitYour measurement is more useful than a guess

Measure your wrist or say “not sure yet” when requesting a recommendation. Use the wrist size guide; fit allowance and final bead count depend on the design.

5. Share a realistic budgetChoose a route before payment, not before asking

Auralis may suggest a currently available style, simple personalization or fully custom work. Fully custom requests generally start around US$60; actual pricing is confirmed after material and design review.

What happens after you send a request?

Send your preferred budget, colors or style, wrist size (or “not sure yet”), gift purpose, delivery country and an optional reference image. Auralis reviews the feasible options and confirms the design direction, price and timing before asking for payment. Do not treat a pictured bracelet as a guaranteed exact match.
