FAQ

Crystal Bracelets by Intention

What is a crystal bracelet by intention?

It begins with a feeling, life chapter or personal reminder rather than a fixed product. Auralis translates that direction into color, material, sizing and design options before quotation.

Can a crystal bracelet provide emotional support?

A bracelet can serve as a symbolic reminder, personal ritual or meaningful object during an emotional period. Auralis does not present jewelry as medical treatment or a guaranteed emotional result.

Which bracelet direction is suitable for calm and grounding?

Calm and grounding directions often use softer neutrals, clear tones, greens, blues or deeper earth colors. The final material and combination depend on the wearer, preferred style, wrist size and budget.

Can I customize a bracelet for a new beginning?

Yes. Share the life change, preferred colors, reference photo, birth date, wrist size or words you want the bracelet to represent. Auralis confirms a symbolic design direction and quotation before payment.

Can I order an intention bracelet as a gift?

Yes. The recipient, occasion, preferred colors, life stage and message can guide the design. Birthday, friendship, encouragement, anniversary and new-chapter requests are welcome.

Do I need to pay before seeing the quotation?

No. Auralis confirms the design direction, available material options, quotation and estimated timeline before payment.
