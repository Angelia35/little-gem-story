Questions, answered

Frequently Asked Questions

A few quick answers before you begin your custom bracelet request.

How does the custom bracelet process work?
Start by sharing what you already know — a color, a story, a birth date, a reference photo or a gift idea. We then help define the design direction and provide a quote before payment.
How much does a custom bracelet cost?
Fully custom bracelets generally start around US$60. Final pricing depends on the selected materials, bead size and design complexity.
Can I customize by birth date or Five Elements?
Yes. Birth date and Five Elements can both be used as personalization inputs together with your style and color preferences.
Can I send a reference photo?
Yes. Reference photos are welcome and can help communicate the overall look, bead scale, color palette or layout you prefer.
What if I do not know which crystals to choose?
That is completely fine. You do not need to know the exact crystal names. A preferred color, mood, gift occasion or simple idea is enough to begin.
Can I order one as a meaningful gift?
Yes. Tell us who it is for, the occasion and any style preferences, and we can help shape a suitable bracelet direction.
