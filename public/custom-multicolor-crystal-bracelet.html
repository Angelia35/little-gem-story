Before You Order

Fit, materials and pricing should be clear

Pictures communicate color and shape; they cannot independently establish exact gem identity, treatment or current inventory. The final design recommendation depends on what can be sourced for your request.

  • Materials: ask for confirmed names and available alternatives before paying. See material disclosure.
  • Size: bead scale and bracelet circumference affect comfort and appearance.
  • Price: material choices, design complexity and accessories can change the quote. There is no fixed price for this reference.
  • Timeline: the proposed production and shipping schedule is confirmed for each order.
  • Design approval: agree on the specific direction, quote and order terms before payment.

Common Questions

Multicolor bracelet FAQs

Can I choose the colors for a multicolor crystal bracelet?

Yes. Share the colors you like, colors to avoid, and whether you prefer a soft or high-contrast mix. Auralis can review a suitable design direction and confirm available materials before quoting.

Do I need to know the exact names of the stones?

No. A reference photo or a description of your preferred colors is enough to begin. Exact gemstone identities, treatments, availability and substitutions are confirmed individually before an order.

How do I choose my bracelet size?

Measure your wrist with a flexible tape or string and share the measurement. You can also explain whether you prefer a close or relaxed fit. Auralis will review the appropriate bead size and fit with you.

Can a multicolor bracelet be a meaningful gift?

Yes. You can describe the recipient, their favorite colors, the occasion and the feeling you want the design to represent. These are personal design choices, not claims of medical or supernatural effects.

When will I receive a price and delivery estimate?

After reviewing your color preferences, wrist size, budget, delivery country and the proposed materials. Prices, production timing, shipping and any design revisions are confirmed with the quote rather than promised on this page.
