Personalization

What can you decide before requesting a quote?

Personalization begins with practical choices, not an abstract promise. The final material and construction options depend on what can be sourced and confirmed for your request.

  • Shade: blush, dusty pink, rosy contrast, or a reference photo you like.
  • Bead appearance: a more delicate or more noticeable proportion, subject to available sizes.
  • Wrist fit: share your wrist measurement so the recommendation is not based on a guess.
  • Occasion: everyday wear, a birthday, a friendship gift, or a personal milestone.
  • Budget and delivery country: help us suggest a realistic direction before quoting.

Common questions

Pink crystal bracelet questions, answered

These answers cover the early design decision. Exact commercial terms are provided with a specific quote.

Read the Wrist Size Guide

Do I need to know which pink gemstone I want?

No. You can start from a color reference. Exact stone identity and any material treatments need to be checked and described before a quote is agreed.

Can I request a softer pink or a mixed-color design?

Yes, describe the shade or mix you prefer and attach a reference link or send an image through the agreed contact channel. We will check feasible materials and designs.

Can I order a pink crystal bracelet as a gift?

You can request a gift direction. Share the occasion, recipient's wearing style, wrist measurement if known, and the delivery country. Available options and timing will be confirmed before purchase.

Is the bracelet in the photo guaranteed to be available?

No. The photos illustrate real style references. The quoted item, exact materials, and availability must be confirmed for your request.

Are pink crystal meanings guaranteed to change how I feel?

No. Any connection between color and emotion is a personal or cultural symbol, not a health treatment or a guaranteed outcome.
