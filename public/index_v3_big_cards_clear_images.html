FAQ

Questions Before You Send a Custom Request

How does a custom crystal bracelet order work?

Send a reference photo, birth date, Chinese element-inspired request, color mood, wrist size or gift idea. We review the request and confirm the design direction, material options, quotation and estimated timeline before payment.

Can I send only a reference photo?

Yes. A photo or screenshot is enough to begin. Preferred colors, wrist size, delivery country and gift purpose can be added if you already know them.

Do I need to pay before the quote is confirmed?

No. Payment is not required before the design direction, material options, quotation and estimated timeline are confirmed.

Are the crystals natural?

Auralis offers natural crystal and gemstone-inspired material options. Material details are confirmed according to the selected design and quotation. Natural stones may vary in color, pattern and texture.

What if I am not sure about my wrist size?

Send an approximate measurement or explain that you are unsure. We will provide sizing guidance before production.

How long does custom jewelry take?

Timing depends on design complexity, material availability, quantity and destination. The estimated crafting and shipping timeline is confirmed before the order moves forward.
